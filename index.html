How the Connected Network Operates

A continuous data loop tracking produce from the soil to the dinner table.

01

Direct Harvest & Mandi Benchmarking

Cultivators register harvest batches with certified quality grades and compare farm-gate asking rates against regional APMC mandi market indices.

02

Cold-Chain Logistics & Degradation Telemetry

Wholesalers track temperature and route vibrations in transit, calculating degradation indices to ensure produce arrives at optimal market freshness.

03

Household Pantry & Zero-Waste Planning

Consumers receive fresh produce with origin confidence, monitor pantry shelf-life with expiry warnings, and cook gourmet meals that minimize waste.
